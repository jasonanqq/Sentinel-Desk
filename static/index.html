LOCAL SEARCH

Search events

Uses the global time range selected above. Supported commands: search, stats, timechart, table, head and sort.

Search language examples
index=network source=fortigate
severity=high OR severity=critical
username=admin | table timestamp src_ip event_type message
index=endpoint | stats count by event_type
index=network | timechart span=1h count
source=windows | sort -timestamp | head 25

Use implicit AND, OR, NOT, quoted terms and * wildcards. Field names include index, source, sourcetype, host, timestamp, severity, event_type, src_ip, dest_ip, username and details.key. This is a limited SPL-inspired syntax—not full Splunk SPL.

Submit an email
Upload an .eml file or paste its complete raw headers

          

          
Attachments and active HTML are never executed.

          
Header-only analysis cannot detect threats expressed solely in the email body.

          
          
Reported SPF, DKIM, DMARC and compauth values are read from supplied headers, not independently verified. Email content stays in your local SQLite database.

        

        
Investigation approach
What the detection engine can and cannot conclude

          
01 · Authentication
Extract reported SPF, DKIM, DMARC and Microsoft compauth verdicts. Uploaded headers may be forged.
02 · Content and context
Detect sextortion, credential requests, urgent pressure, suspicious links and sender identity mismatches.
03 · Explainable decisions
Each indicator has a rule ID, a weighted score, a rationale and its evidence source.
04 · Investigation workflow
Review stored headers, update case status and retain a stable investigation ID.
